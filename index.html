The game of the week

Ohio State at Texas, 6:30 on ABC, is a pick'em — Texas by 1.5. Third meeting between them in 20 months; Ohio State won the other two, including 14-7 in Columbus last August. The total sits at just 48.5 for that reason.

Road favorites, and there are a lot

Eleven visitors are laying points, including Oklahoma at Michigan (−6), Oregon at Oklahoma State (−22.5), Alabama at Kentucky (−10), Tennessee at Georgia Tech (−13.5) and Penn State at Temple (−23.5).

Two FCS teams favored on the road

North Dakota State is a 3-point favorite at Air Force and Montana State lays 5.5 at Nevada. Three FCS teams already beat FBS opponents in Week 1.

About the numbers
